Opponents

The engines gauntlets are played against: each with the repository and release it comes from and the rating its list gave it, read once by hand from the page it links to. A row appears when a gauntlet reports an engine, or when it has a repository and a tag. Ours is the rating fitted from every gauntlet game we have played, on the list's scale, under our conditions (hardware, book, hash), with its one-sigma error and the games it rests on; it is not the list's rating. A list rating is a prior for it, so an opponent we have played about a hundred games stays near it, and a flagged row is one our games disagree with by more than twice the error. Two names for one engine are merged{% if not may_edit %}; sign in to edit or merge{% endif %}.
